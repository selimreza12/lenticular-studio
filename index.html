Lenticular Studio Reference & Guide

Optical Interlacing Formula

Lenticular prints work by slicing multiple images into fine microscopic strips positioned under cylindrical lenticules. The formula for the individual stripe width is:

Stripe Width (pixels) = Printer DPI / (Lens LPI × 2 views)

Standard Workflow

  1. Upload View A and View B: Image A defines the master output aspect ratio.
  2. Adjust Normalization: Select Cover, Contain, or Stretch and fine-tune offset/scale.
  3. Interactive Simulation: Move your mouse across the canvas or enable Auto-Loop to verify alignment and seamless switching.
  4. Set DPI & LPI: Match your printer driver hardware resolution (e.g. 600 or 1200 DPI) and physical sheet rating (e.g. 75 LPI).
  5. Render & Download: Generate the lossless, uncompressed PNG file ready for lamination.

Direction Guidelines

Vertical Lenticules (Left-Right): Recommended for 3D stereoscopic depth. The viewer's eyes receive different views simultaneously.

Horizontal Lenticules (Top-Bottom): Recommended for 2-image flip/morph graphics viewed when tilted by hand.
